# Privacy and third-party services

[Back to Linory](../README.md)

For each track lookup that is not cached, the app sends the song title, artist, album, and duration to LRCLIB over HTTPS. LRCLIB receives the request and associated network metadata, such as your IP address. Playback details are read locally from Spotify or Music through macOS Automation. For Spotify tracks, the cover art is downloaded from the image link Spotify provides; Music provides its cover art locally. Settings and the window position are stored locally in macOS preferences. Lookup results are cached in `~/Library/Application Support/io.github.mikeleonfox.lyricsoverlay/lyrics-cache.json` (up to 500 tracks; "not found" results are retried after 7 days). Delete that file to clear the cache. No application analytics are implemented in this prototype.

Connecting Spotify is optional. When connected, Linory talks to `accounts.spotify.com` and `api.spotify.com` over HTTPS using the Client ID of your own Spotify app. The Client ID is stored in macOS preferences. The refresh token is stored in the macOS Keychain (service `io.github.mikeleonfox.lyricsoverlay`, account `spotify-refresh-token`); the short-lived access token is kept only in memory. Tokens are never logged. **Disconnect** in Settings deletes the Keychain item.

True Shuffle reads your Liked Songs and the playlists you own or collaborate on, and writes the private **Linory Shuffle** playlist (and any playlist you choose to keep) to your Spotify account. Shuffle & play asks Spotify for your devices and starts the playlist on this Mac's Spotify app. During a shuffle, Linory asks Spotify for the playing context once per track change, to check that the track plays from the Linory Shuffle playlist. When **Keep playing** is on, Linory also reads the source again and appends the next round to the Linory Shuffle playlist near the end of each round. Shuffle progress is stored in `~/Library/Application Support/io.github.mikeleonfox.lyricsoverlay/shuffle-state.json`: the track URIs of played and removed tracks per source (a mix of sources is stored under an ID made of its members), the current order with each track's title, main artist and artist ID (for **Up next** and for keeping artists apart), the position in that order, and the ID of the Linory Shuffle playlist. Delete that file, with Linory quit, to clear it. The **Keep playing** setting is stored in macOS preferences.

**Listening history:** while Linory runs, it records each song you play in Spotify or Music (title, artist, album, duration, the Spotify track URI, when it started, how many seconds actually played, and whether synced lyrics were shown) in `~/Library/Application Support/io.github.mikeleonfox.lyricsoverlay/history.jsonl`. It stays on this Mac and is never uploaded. Turn it off or clear it at the bottom of the **Your music** window, or delete the file with Linory quit.

The **On Spotify** section of **Your music** reads your top artists and tracks from Spotify (`GET /me/top`, scope `user-top-read`) when you open it, once per time range while Linory runs. They are kept in memory only, never saved. The sleep timer runs locally and sends only a pause command to Spotify or Music.

The mini-player's heart asks Spotify once per Spotify track whether it is in your Liked Songs (`GET /me/library/contains`), and adds or removes it only when you press the heart. Transport commands, seeking and opening a track in Spotify go to the player on this Mac through Automation or a `spotify:` link, not over the network.
